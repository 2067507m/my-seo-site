Crafting an Impactful Claims Adjuster Resume: A Step-by-Step Guide

A well‑structured claims adjuster resume can set you apart from the competition, showcasing your analytical skills, customer‑service expertise, and industry knowledge. This guide offers actionable tips, keyword‑rich content, and formatting suggestions to help you create a resume that recruiters will love.

Highlight Your Core Competencies

Start your resume with a concise summary that highlights the most relevant skills for a claims adjuster role. Focus on measurable achievements and industry‑specific terminology.

  • Claims Analysis – Proven ability to assess damages, estimate repair costs, and negotiate settlements.
  • Investigative Skills – Experienced in gathering evidence, interviewing witnesses, and verifying policy coverage.
  • Regulatory Knowledge – Familiarity with state insurance laws, fraud detection protocols, and compliance standards.
  • Communication – Strong written and verbal skills; adept at preparing detailed reports for internal stakeholders and clients.

Showcase Your Professional Experience

Use a reverse‑chronological format to detail your past roles. Quantify your impact with numbers, percentages, or time‑savings whenever possible.

Senior Claims Adjuster – XYZ Insurance, New York, NY
June 2019 – Present

  • Managed a portfolio of 300+ claims, reducing average settlement time by 15% through process optimization.
  • Conducted field inspections, producing comprehensive reports that increased customer satisfaction scores to 4.8/5.
  • Trained 12 junior adjusters on investigative techniques and regulatory compliance, boosting team efficiency by 20%.

Claims Adjuster – ABC Insurance, Brooklyn, NY
March 2015 – May 2019

  • Reviewed and processed 150+ claims monthly, maintaining a 98% accuracy rate.
  • Implemented a new electronic filing system that cut administrative overhead by 12%.
  • Collaborated with legal counsel on complex fraud investigations, resulting in a 30% reduction in fraudulent payouts.

Education, Certifications & Technical Skills

Include any relevant degrees, certifications, and software proficiencies that strengthen your candidacy.

  • Bachelor of Science in Risk Management – State University, 2014
  • Certified Claims Adjuster (CCA) – National Association of Insurance Adjusters, 2016
  • Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE) – Association of Certified Fraud Examiners, 2018
  • Software Proficiency – ClaimsCenter, Guidewire, Microsoft Office Suite, Adobe Acrobat

Tailor Your Resume for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)

To ensure your claims adjuster resume passes ATS filters, incorporate industry keywords naturally throughout the document. Avoid excessive jargon and use clear, action‑oriented language.

  • Keywords to Include: “claims investigation,” “damage assessment,” “settlement negotiation,” “policy compliance,” “fraud detection,” “risk analysis,” “customer service,” “report writing,” “state insurance regulations.”

Conclusion

A polished claims adjuster resume that balances concise content with quantifiable achievements can dramatically increase your chances of landing an interview. Remember to keep the layout clean, use bullet points for readability, and tailor each application to the specific job posting.
