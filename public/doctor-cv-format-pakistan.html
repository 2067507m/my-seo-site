Doctor CV Format Pakistan: A Step‑by‑Step Guide

A well‑structured CV can set a medical professional apart in a competitive job market. In Pakistan, where many hospitals and private practices require detailed documentation, having a clear, concise, and ATS‑friendly doctor CV format is essential. This guide walks you through the key sections, formatting tips, and content that will make your CV stand out to recruiters and hiring managers.

1. Contact Information & Professional Summary

  • Full name, qualifications, and registration number (e.g., MBBS, FCPS, or MD)
  • Phone number, email, and LinkedIn profile
  • Professional summary: 2–3 sentences highlighting your specialty, years of experience, and career goals

Tip: Keep the summary concise and tailored to the position you’re applying for. Mention any unique skills or achievements that align with the hospital’s needs.

2. Education & Training

List your academic background in reverse chronological order:

  • Degree (e.g., MBBS, MD, MSc in Public Health) – University, City, Year of graduation
  • Post‑graduate training (e.g., FCPS in Internal Medicine, Fellowship in Cardiology) – Institute, City, Year
  • Additional certifications (e.g., Advanced Cardiac Life Support, Basic Life Support)

Bullet Point Example
• MBBS, University of Karachi, 2014
• FCPS, Internal Medicine, Allama Iqbal Medical College, 2019

3. Professional Experience

Highlight positions relevant to the job:

  • Job title – Hospital/Clinic name, City, Dates
  • Key responsibilities: Use action verbs (diagnosed, treated, managed)
  • Achievements: Quantify results when possible (e.g., reduced patient wait time by 20%)

Bullet Point Example
• Managed a 30‑patient daily caseload, achieving a 95% patient satisfaction score
• Implemented a new electronic health record system, improving documentation efficiency by 35%

4. Skills & Competencies

Separate hard and soft skills:

  • Clinical skills: e.g., ECG interpretation, surgical procedures, patient counseling
  • Technical skills: EHR systems (Epic, Cerner), data analysis, telemedicine platforms
  • Languages: Urdu, English, Punjabi (if applicable)

Bullet Point Example
• Proficient in Epic EHR and Microsoft Office Suite
• Fluent in Urdu and English

5. Publications & Conferences

List peer‑reviewed articles, conference presentations, and workshops:

  • Author(s) – Title – Journal/Conference – Year
  • Presentations – Topic – Venue – Date

Bullet Point Example
• “Impact of Early Intervention in Pediatric Asthma,” Journal of Medicine, 2022

6. Professional Memberships & Licenses

  • Medical Council of Pakistan (MCP) – Registration number
  • Pakistan Medical Association (PMA) – Member since 2015
  • International organizations (if any)

7. References

  • Provide 2–3 professional references or state “Available upon request.”
  • Include name, title, institution, and contact details.

Conclusion

A clear, concise, and well‑structured doctor CV format Pakistan should showcase your qualifications, experience, and unique contributions. By following the sections above, using bullet points for readability, and tailoring content to each job, you’ll create a compelling document that captures recruiters’ attention.
