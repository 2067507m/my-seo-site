How to Craft a Standout Quantity Surveyor Resume

A quantity surveyor resume is more than a list of skills; it’s a strategic document that showcases your expertise in cost control, project management, and value engineering. In a competitive market, a well‑structured resume can open doors to senior roles, consultancy gigs, and international projects. Below, we outline the essential components, formatting tips, and content ideas that will help you create a resume that attracts recruiters and ATS systems alike.

1. Start With a Powerful Summary

Your summary is the first impression recruiters will form. Keep it concise (3–4 lines) and focus on:

  • Professional identity (e.g., “Certified Quantity Surveyor with 8+ years in commercial construction”)
  • Key achievements (e.g., “Reduced project costs by 12% through strategic sourcing”)
  • Career goal (e.g., “Seeking a senior role in cost management for large-scale infrastructure projects”)

This section should contain the keyword naturally: “quantity surveyor resume”.

2. Highlight Core Competencies

Use bullet points to list the technical and soft skills that set you apart. Group them into categories for clarity.

  • Cost Management & Estimation
    • Detailed cost planning, bill of quantities, and cost forecasting
    • Proficiency in MS Excel, CostX, and Oracle Primavera
  • Contract Administration
    • Expertise in JCT, NEC, and FIDIC contracts
    • Experience with variation orders and dispute resolution
  • Project Leadership
    • Cross‑functional team coordination, stakeholder communication
    • Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
  • Sustainability & Value Engineering
    • LEED and BREEAM certification processes
    • Life‑cycle cost analysis and green procurement

3. Showcase Quantifiable Achievements

Numbers speak louder than words. Quantify your impact wherever possible:

  • Managed budgets up to £12 million, consistently delivering projects under budget by an average of 8%.
  • Negotiated supplier contracts that saved £350,000 annually.
  • Implemented a digital cost‑tracking system that reduced reporting time by 40%.
  • Led a multidisciplinary team of 12 professionals on a high‑rise development project.

These metrics demonstrate your value and align with recruiters’ expectations for a quantity surveyor resume.

4. Detail Your Professional Experience

Structure each role with the following format:

Job Title – Company, City | Dates
- Key responsibility or project
- Achievement with quantified outcome
- Relevant tools or methodologies used

Example:

Senior Quantity Surveyor – XYZ Construction, London | Jan 2018 – Present

  • Oversaw cost control for a £25 million mixed‑use development.
  • Reduced material cost overruns by 15% through proactive supplier negotiations.
  • Integrated BIM cost estimation tools, improving accuracy by 20%.

5. Include Education & Certifications

List degrees, diplomas, and professional certifications relevant to quantity surveying:

  • BSc (Hons) Construction Management – University of Manchester (2014)
  • Chartered Quantity Surveyor (CQS) – Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (2016)
  • Certified Cost Engineer (CCE) – Association for the Advancement of Cost Engineering (2019)

Conclusion

A polished quantity surveyor resume that blends concise summaries, bullet‑pointed competencies, and measurable achievements will set you apart in a crowded job market. Remember to tailor each application to the specific role, incorporating relevant keywords and industry jargon. Keep the layout clean, use action verbs, and proofread meticulously to avoid errors that could cost you an interview.
