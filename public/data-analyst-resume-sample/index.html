Data Analyst Resume Sample: Crafting a Winning CV That Stands Out

A well‑structured data analyst resume showcases your analytical prowess, technical skills, and business impact. This guide gives you a polished sample, key sections to include, and tips for tailoring your CV to any data‑heavy role.

1. Header & Professional Summary

Your header should be clear and concise:

  • Name
  • Phone | Email | LinkedIn | GitHub
  • Location (optional)

Follow with a 2‑3 sentence summary that highlights:

  • Years of experience in data analysis or related fields
  • Core technical expertise (SQL, Python, Tableau, R)
  • Proven impact (e.g., “increased forecasting accuracy by 15%”)

Example: “Data Analyst with 4+ years of experience transforming raw data into actionable insights using SQL, Python, and Tableau. Adept at predictive modeling, data visualization, and cross‑functional collaboration to drive business growth.”

2. Core Competencies

List your most relevant skills in bullet form so recruiters can scan quickly:

  • Programming & Query Languages: SQL, Python (pandas, NumPy), R, SAS
  • Data Visualization: Tableau, Power BI, Looker, matplotlib
  • Statistical Analysis: Regression, hypothesis testing, A/B testing
  • Database Management: MySQL, PostgreSQL, Snowflake, BigQuery
  • Data Cleaning & ETL: Apache Airflow, dbt, Talend
  • Business Intelligence: KPI development, dashboard design, data storytelling
  • Soft Skills: Problem solving, stakeholder communication, project management

3. Professional Experience

For each role, structure it as:

  • Job Title – Company, City – Month/Year – Month/Year
  • Key Responsibilities (bullet points)
  • Quantifiable Achievements (bullet points)

Example

Data Analyst – Acme Retail, New York, NY – Jan 2021 – Present

  • Designed and maintained interactive Tableau dashboards tracking sales, inventory, and customer segments, reducing reporting time by 40%.
  • Conducted cohort analysis to identify churn drivers, informing a retention strategy that lifted repeat purchase rates by 12%.
  • Automated weekly data pipelines using SQL and Airflow, improving data freshness and reliability.
  • Collaborated with marketing to execute A/B tests, yielding a 5% lift in campaign ROI.

Tips

  • Use action verbs: “analyzed,” “optimized,” “implemented.”
  • Quantify results with percentages, dollar amounts, or time savings.
  • Align achievements with the job description’s keywords.

4. Education & Certifications

  • Degree – University, City – Month/Year
  • Relevant Coursework: Statistical Modeling, Data Mining, Machine Learning
  • Certifications: Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate, Tableau Desktop Specialist, Microsoft Certified: Data Analyst Associate

5. Projects & Portfolio

Showcase personal or freelance projects that demonstrate your skill set:

  • Customer Segmentation Tool – Built a Python‑based clustering model to segment users, improving targeted marketing spend by 20%.
  • Sales Forecasting Dashboard – Developed a live Power BI dashboard forecasting quarterly sales with a 92% accuracy rate.
  • Open‑Source Contribution – Contributed to the pandas library, improving data frame performance by 15%.

Provide links to GitHub repositories or a personal website.

Conclusion

A data analyst resume is more than a list of duties—it’s a narrative of how you turn data into decisions. Use clear sections, quantifiable achievements, and industry keywords to capture hiring managers’ attention. Tailor each submission to the specific role, and keep the design clean and professional.
