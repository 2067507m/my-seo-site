# Salesforce Developer Resume: A Complete Guide to Crafting a Winning CV

A well‑structured **salesforce developer resume** can set you apart in a competitive job market. This guide breaks down the essential elements, showcases best practices, and offers actionable tips to help you land your next role.

## Core Sections Every Salesforce Developer Resume Should Include

- **Contact Information** – Name, phone, email, LinkedIn, and GitHub (if applicable).  
- **Professional Summary** – A 2‑sentence snapshot of your experience, key strengths, and career goals.  
- **Technical Skills** – List Salesforce‑specific technologies, programming languages, and tools.  
- **Experience** – Chronological work history with bullet‑pointed achievements.  
- **Education & Certifications** – Degrees, relevant coursework, and Salesforce certifications.  
- **Projects & Portfolio** – Highlight side projects, contributions, and live demos.

## Highlighting Technical Skills and Certifications

| Category | Key Items |
|----------|-----------|
| **Salesforce Platforms** | Apex, Visualforce, Lightning Web Components (LWC), Flow, Process Builder |
| **Development Tools** | VS Code, Salesforce CLI, Git, Heroku |
| **Databases & Integration** | SOQL, SOSL, REST/SOAP APIs, MuleSoft |
| **Soft Skills** | Agile methodology, problem‑solving, cross‑functional collaboration |

*Tip:* Group skills by proficiency level and use action verbs like “implemented,” “optimized,” and “integrated” to describe your contributions.
