## Tips for Customizing Your Resume for the Pakistani Market

- **Highlight Local Certifications** – Include degrees from recognized Pakistani universities and local certifications (e.g., CPEC, ITU).  
- **Use Local Terminology** – Reference local industries, such as “Telecom (PTCL)” or “Finance (SBI)”.  
- **Show Multilingual Skills** – List proficiency in Urdu, English, and any other languages.  
- **Add Volunteer Experience** – Many employers value community involvement, especially in NGOs and local projects.  
- **Quantify Achievements** – Numbers resonate: “Increased sales by 30% in Q4 2023”.  

These nuances make your resume relatable to hiring managers across Pakistan.
