## How to Create a Winning CV Using an Online Tool

1. **Choose the Right Template**  
   Start with a clean, professional layout that aligns with your industry—tech, finance, or academia.

2. **Fill in Your Personal Details**  
   Include name, phone number, email, LinkedIn URL, and optionally a professional photo.

3. **Craft a Compelling Summary**  
   Write a 2‑sentence overview highlighting your experience, skills, and career goals.

4. **List Your Experience**  
   Use bullet points, quantify achievements, and start each point with an action verb.

5. **Highlight Education & Certifications**  
   Emphasize local degrees, scholarships, and relevant certifications (e.g., BCS, PMP).

6. **Showcase Technical & Soft Skills**  
   Separate hard and soft skills to give recruiters a clear skill map.

7. **Add Projects & Publications**  
   Include links to GitHub, research papers, or portfolio sites.

8. **Proofread & Optimize**  
   Check for spelling errors, consistent formatting, and keyword optimization for ATS.

9. **Export & Submit**  
   Download the final PDF or Word file, then upload it to job portals like Rozee.pk or LinkedIn.

## Tips for Tailoring Your CV to the Pakistani Job Market

- **Use Local Terminology**: Replace generic terms with locally recognized titles (e.g., “Assistant Manager” instead of “Assistant Manager”).
- **Include Relevant Certifications**: Highlight certifications from Pakistani institutions or recognized bodies.
- **Add a “Languages” Section**: List proficiency in Urdu, English, and any regional languages.
- **Mention Soft Skills Valued in Pakistan**: Teamwork, communication, and adaptability are highly prized.
- **Add Volunteer Work**: Show community involvement, which resonates with many Pakistani employers.
