# How to Make a Resume on Laptop: A Step‑by‑Step Guide

Creating a professional resume from scratch on your laptop has never been easier. Whether you’re a recent graduate, a seasoned professional, or just looking to refresh your CV, this guide will walk you through the entire process—from choosing the right software to exporting a polished final product.

## 1. Choose the Right Software

Selecting the right tool sets the foundation for a clean, effective resume. Pick a program that balances ease of use with powerful formatting options.

- **Microsoft Word** – Industry‑standard, extensive templates, robust formatting.
- **Google Docs** – Free, cloud‑based, easy collaboration, consistent formatting across devices.
- **Adobe InDesign** – Ideal for creative roles that require a unique visual style.
- **Resume builders (e.g., JadeAI, Canva, Novoresume)** – Guided templates, AI‑powered content suggestions.

### Tips
- Ensure the software supports PDF export to preserve formatting.
- Check for built‑in spell‑check and grammar tools.

## 2. Gather Your Information

Before you open a new document, compile all the data you’ll need. This saves time and keeps your resume focused.

- **Contact details** – Name, phone number, professional email, LinkedIn URL.
- **Professional summary** – 2‑3 sentence snapshot of your experience and goals.
- **Work experience** – Company, role, dates, key achievements (use bullet points, action verbs, metrics).
- **Education** – Degrees, institutions, graduation dates, relevant coursework.
- **Skills** – Technical, soft, and language skills.
- **Certifications & Awards** – Include dates and issuing bodies.
- **Volunteer & Extracurriculars** – Relevant to the role or industry.

> **Pro tip:** Use a spreadsheet to organize your work history and achievements; copy and paste into your resume later.

## 3. Design Your Resume Layout

A well‑structured layout enhances readability and showcases your strengths at a glance.

| Section | Placement | Key Elements |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Header | Top | Name, phone, email, LinkedIn |
| Summary | Below header | 2‑3 sentence career overview |
| Experience | Middle | Reverse chronological order |
| Education | Near bottom | Highest degree first |
| Skills | Sidebar or separate section | Bullet list or skill bar |
| Certifications | Optional | Icons or badges |

### Formatting Checklist
- **Font**: Sans‑serif (Calibri, Arial, Helvetica) or serif (Times New Roman) – 10‑12 pt for body, 14‑16 pt for headings.
- **Margins**: 0.75–1 inch all sides.
- **Alignment**: Left‑justified for body text; centered for header.
- **Bullet points**: Use simple dashes or round bullets; keep each point under 10 words if possible.
- **White space**: Enough to avoid clutter; aim for a clean look.

## 4. Polish, Proofread, and Export

A flawless resume is as important as its content. Follow these final steps to ensure perfection.

- **Spell‑check & Grammar** – Run through built‑in tools and a second review.
- **Consistent tense** – Past tense for past roles, present tense for current roles.
- **Quantify achievements** – Use numbers, percentages, or dollar amounts.
- **File format** – Save as PDF for universal compatibility.
- **File name** – `FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf`.

### Quick Proofreading Checklist
- No typos or grammatical errors.
- No duplicate information.
- All dates align with your CV.
- Contact details are up‑to‑date.

## Conclusion

Creating a polished resume on your laptop is a straightforward process when you follow a clear plan: pick the right tool, gather all necessary data, design an organized layout, and polish until it’s perfect. With these steps, you’ll produce a resume that stands out to recruiters and showcases your professional brand.
