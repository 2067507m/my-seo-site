# Crafting a Stand‑Out PHP Developer Resume

A **PHP developer resume** is your first step toward landing the job you want. It must showcase your technical expertise, project experience, and soft skills while remaining concise and easy to scan. Below is a proven framework that will help you create a professional, SEO‑friendly resume that recruiters can’t ignore.

## 1. Essential Sections to Include

| Section | Why It Matters | What to Highlight |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Contact Information** | Quick access for hiring managers | Name, phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub |
| **Professional Summary** | Sets the tone | 2‑3 sentence overview of experience, specialty in PHP, career goals |
| **Core Competencies** | Keyword‑rich for ATS | PHP, Laravel, Symfony, REST APIs, MySQL, Git, Docker |
| **Work Experience** | Proof of impact | Company name, role, dates, bullet‑point achievements |
| **Projects** | Demonstrates hands‑on skills | Open‑source contributions, freelance work, personal apps |
| **Education & Certifications** | Credentials | Degree, institution, relevant courses, PHP certifications |
| **Additional Sections** | Optional but valuable | Languages, awards, volunteer work |

> **Tip:** Keep the resume to one page if you have less than 10 years of experience; two pages are acceptable for seasoned professionals.

## 2. Highlighting Technical Skills

Your **technical skill set** must be front‑and‑center to pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). Use a two‑column list for clarity:

- **Backend**: PHP 7/8, Composer, PSR‑4, MVC frameworks (Laravel, Symfony, CodeIgniter)
- **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Vue.js, React
- **Databases**: MySQL, PostgreSQL, Redis, MongoDB
- **Tools & DevOps**: Git, GitHub Actions, Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, PHPUnit
- **Cloud & Hosting**: AWS (EC2, S3, RDS), DigitalOcean, Azure, Netlify
- **Testing & Security**: TDD, BDD, OWASP, OWASP ZAP

> **SEO Boost:** Sprinkle the exact phrase “PHP developer resume” and related keywords throughout the skills and summary sections.

## 3. Formatting Tips for Readability

- **Bullet Points:** Use action verbs (e.g., *developed*, *optimized*, *integrated*) and quantify results (e.g., *reduced page load time by 35%*).
- **Consistent Date Format:** `MM/YYYY – MM/YYYY` or `Month Year – Present`.
- **Font & Margins:** 10–12 pt font (Calibri, Garamond, or Helvetica); 0.5‑in margins.
- **Avoid Graphics:** ATS cannot read images; stick to text.
- **File Name:** `Firstname_Lastname_PHP_Developer_Resume.pdf`.

## 4. Showcasing Impactful Projects

Include a dedicated section for projects that illustrate your PHP expertise. For each project, provide:

1. **Project Name & Link** (GitHub, live demo)
2. **Role** (Lead developer, contributor)
3. **Tech Stack** (PHP, Laravel, Vue, MySQL, Docker)
4. **Key Achievements** (e.g., *implemented a custom CMS that handled 10k+ concurrent users*)

> **Example:**
> - *E‑commerce Platform*: Built a Laravel‑based storefront with Stripe integration, boosting sales conversion by 20%.

## Conclusion

A well‑structured **PHP developer resume** balances technical depth with clear, concise presentation. By following the sections above, optimizing for keywords, and showcasing tangible results, you’ll increase your chances of landing interviews and securing your next role.
